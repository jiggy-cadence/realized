The cause

Advertised APR has no price term. It cannot show a loss.

Advertised APR is fees ÷ liquidity, annualized. Fees are never negative — so the number can never be.

advertised = fees only → always ≥ 0
realized   = fees + impermanent loss → the actual answer

The missing term is what the pool cost you by rebalancing out of the asset that went up.

LPs losing to HODL is not our finding — Topaze Blue / Bancor, 2021. Ours is calibrating the advertised metric itself, pool by pool, against what happened.
